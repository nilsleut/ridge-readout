# Step 48b: amplitude dominance of the RDMs. Predictions (fixed before scripts and runs)
Date: 2026-11-22. Configuration: config_step48b.yaml, config hash 73c5a25219bb.
Facts known before: step 48 (NB1, NB2, NB3, G1 met; G2, G3 not met: per-image amplitude is matched about equally by both networks). Before this amendment, a simulation with synthetic patterns (no real data) showed that the difference form |g_i − g_j| does not capture the amplitude structure of correlation-distance RDMs under a common profile, whereas the sum form does; the definition was chosen on that basis.
Hypothesis: with a common profile left in, both RDMs are dominated by per-image amplitude structure; the untrained network's RDM is more amplitude-dominated than the trained network's, which makes it more similar to the brain RDM although its amplitude match is not better.
Definitions: amplitude RDM A_ij = 2 − g_i − g_j (brain, per convention and subject) and A^M_ij = 2 − g^M_i − g^M_j (model, per seed); this sum form follows from step 48, test (ii): with a common profile left in, two patterns are similar when both have high amplitude along the profile. g and g^M as in step 48. Paper cell: raw units, correlation distance, GAP Conv1, V1; pair sets as in step 48; Δ = Random − BP, mean over seeds 0–4, then subjects. Two-sided partial: brain RDM residualised on A, model RDM residualised on A^M (rank-based, as in step 44), Spearman of the residuals.
D1 (model side): Spearman(model RDM, A^M) is larger for Random than for BP in 5 of 5 seeds, in each of THINGS-720, THINGS test images and NSD-515.
D2 (brain side): Spearman(brain RDM, A) is larger under T-raw than under T-session in each THINGS-720 subject, and larger under N-raw than under N-session (k = 1) in at least 7 of 8 NSD subjects.
D3 (bridge): in NSD under N-raw (k = 1), the two-sided partial reduces Δ from +0.1165 to ≤ +0.020, with Δ ≤ +0.020 in at least 6 of 8 subjects.
Interpretation fixed in advance:
  D1, D2 and D3 met → the untrained advantage under conventions that leave a common profile in both patterns is attributed to amplitude structure dominating both RDMs, with the untrained network's RDM more amplitude-dominated; not to a better match of per-image amplitude.
  Any other pattern → not identified; all values are reported.
Reported without prediction: the same quantities under all conventions and k; THINGS-720 and THINGS test images for D3; LO-obj; the Spearman of model amplitude dominance with the moment ratio of arXiv:2611.15533 across the 10 checkpoints; the difference form |g_i − g_j| for all quantities, for comparison.
