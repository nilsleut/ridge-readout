This exploratory analysis was added after a second critical review of a revised draft; its
rule was committed before its script (Section~\ref{sec:inference}). The mean-profile
decomposition of Section~\ref{sec:gainmethods} was applied to the model side, the brain
side, both or neither. Table~\ref{tab:ablation2x2} gives the V1 contrast at NATIVE 224\,px
(raw GAP features, correlation distance, $B$ = paper) for the untrained (uncalibrated BN
statistics) network minus Backprop in all four combinations, and for the six ImageNet models
(untrained, mean of three initialisations, minus trained, 224\,px) in the two mixed
combinations. All values use the same 1000 stimulus-bootstrap resamples. The full/full and
orthogonal/orthogonal cells reproduce the full and orthogonal variants of
Section~\ref{sec:ablation}. In the ImageNet models, the CI lay above 0 in
\AbTwoImnOrthoFullPos{} and below 0 in \AbTwoImnOrthoFullNeg{} models with the orthogonal
model against the full brain RDM, and above 0 in \AbTwoImnFullOrthoPos{} and below 0 in
\AbTwoImnFullOrthoNeg{} models with the full model against the orthogonal brain RDM
(descriptive, no rule).
