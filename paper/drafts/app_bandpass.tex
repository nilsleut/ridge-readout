The band-pass inputs were built from the image after resizing and centre-cropping to
224\,px and before the channel normalisation. Gaussian low-pass filters $L_r$ with
$r \in \{112, 56, 28, 14\}$ have an amplitude response of 0.5 at $r/2$ cycles per image; the
octave bands are differences of consecutive filters. Band 0--14 is the coarse residual $L_{14}$ and
contains the image mean. Condition ``only 0--14'' keeps this band alone, and condition ``notch
0--14'' is the native image minus this band, shifted by the CIFAR-10 channel-mean grey. Both were
evaluated at NATIVE 224\,px with all 30 checkpoints.

Table~\ref{tab:bandgap} gives the V1 contrast (untrained minus Backprop) and the LOC contrast
(Backprop minus untrained) with raw and channel-z-scored features, with 95\% stimulus-bootstrap
intervals. The V1 contrast with the coarse band alone was positive under raw features, as for
native input ($+\VoneGapRawTwoTwoFour$); without the coarse band it was negative.
Table~\ref{tab:bandm} gives the moment ratio $m$ of Section~\ref{sec:standardization}, mean over
seeds. The prediction P\_mech2 that relates $m$ to the difference between raw and z-scored RSA
was not met (Appendix~\ref{app:failed}).
