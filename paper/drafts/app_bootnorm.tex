In the main analyses, unit means and SDs, the feature transformations $F$, the voxel
normalisations $B$, the mean profile and the norms are estimated once on the 720 stimuli,
and the bootstrap resamples pairs of the resulting fixed RDMs (Section~\ref{sec:rsa}). In this
exploratory check, the statistics were re-estimated from the resampled rows of each of the
first 200 resamples (duplicates kept, self-pairs dropped), and the RDMs were rebuilt with
them. Table~\ref{tab:bootnorm} compares the V1 contrast of the untrained (uncalibrated BN
statistics) network minus Backprop at NATIVE 224\,px in three cells. The classification of
the interval relative to 0 was the same in all three cells (BN1: met). With euclidean
distance, centring does not change the distances, so the two intervals agree by construction
over the same resamples.
