Run-grouped cross-validation was an exploratory sensitivity analysis of sub-01
(\NReliableSubOne{} reliable V1 voxels). It was not part of the pre-specified plan, which fixes
random folds. The 720 stimuli come from 10 runs of 72. The 10 runs were permuted (seed 20260818)
and consecutive pairs of runs formed the 5 outer test folds. Within each outer training set, the
8 remaining runs were permuted (seed $20260818 + 1000\,(k + 1)$ for outer fold $k$) and
consecutive pairs formed the 4 inner folds. Standardisation on training rows, the regularisation
grid, the selection rule and the gate were unchanged. All 30 checkpoints were fitted at
$g \in \{1, 4, 8\}$.

Table~\ref{tab:runfolds} gives the untrained (uncalibrated BN statistics) minus Backprop
contrast, mean $\pm$ SD over the 5 seeds with the number of positive seeds. The contrast was
negative in every seed under both fold schemes. Run-grouped folds lowered the pooled $r$ of all 30
checkpoints by $\RunFoldLevelGOne$, $\RunFoldLevelGFour$ and $\RunFoldLevelGEight$ at
$g = 1, 4, 8$. Table~\ref{tab:encrsa} gives the contrast of encoding-RSA, in which the
out-of-fold predictions of the 501 voxels form a model RDM that is compared with the fMRI RDM by
the RSA statistic of Section~\ref{sec:rsa}; ``centred'' denotes predictions centred within each
outer fold.
