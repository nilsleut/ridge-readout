\subsection{Follow-up analyses}
\label{sec:followup}

After version~1 we ran \AmendmentsFollowup{} further steps (Appendix~\ref{app:chronology}, Table~\ref{tab:chronology}). As before,
each step's predictions, decision rules and interpretation rules were committed before its scripts were
written and run; where new data were needed, also before the data were loaded. Two orderings concern
the NSD data. The predictions Q1--Q4 (step 42b) were written after the subj01 pilot had shown a
positive Euclidean contrast and before any data of subj02--08 were loaded (first beta of subj02
requested at \FirstDownloadSubjTwo, after the amendment commit), and they are evaluated on subj02--08
only. The amendment of step 48, which predicted the effect of the normalisation convention in NSD
(NB1, NB2), was committed before the first raw NSD beta was downloaded (\FirstDownloadRawFortyEight).
The definition of the amplitude RDM in step 48b was chosen after a simulation on synthetic patterns,
before the amendment and before any dominance value was computed on real data
(Section~\ref{sec:ampmethods}).

Each interpretation rule is reported with its literal outcome. The rule of step 48 has no case for the
pattern that occurred (NB1 and NB2 met, G2 or G3 not met); its outcome is reported as ``no statement of
the rule applies'', and the amendment was not changed. All predictions, with wording, commit, result
and source file, are listed in \texttt{paper/PREDICTIONS\_LEDGER.csv}; the predictions that were not
met are in Appendix~\ref{app:failed}.
