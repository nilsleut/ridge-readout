\subsection{NSD}
\label{sec:nsdmethods}

\paragraph{Data.} The Natural Scenes Dataset \citep{allen2022} contains 7T fMRI of 8 subjects who
viewed natural scenes. We used the 515 images that every subject saw three times; all their trials lie
in sessions 1--30. Single-trial betas (\texttt{betas\_fithrf\_GLMdenoise\_RR}, 1.8\,mm) were divided by
300, which gives percent signal change, and z-scored per voxel within session, the convention of the
NSD documentation (N-session, Section~\ref{sec:convmethods}). Voxels invalid in any session were
excluded (in one subject). The image order of the features equals that of the betas for
every subject.

\paragraph{ROIs.} V1 is the union of V1v and V1d of the population-receptive-field ROIs. NSD provides no
object-selective ROI; we constructed LO-obj, fixed before any RSA, as the intersection of the
\texttt{lateral} stream ROI with voxels whose \texttt{floc\_objectstval} exceeds 0, the threshold
convention of the manual for the floc ROIs. The underlying contrast is the objects domain (car,
instrument) against all other categories of the functional localiser (characters, bodies, faces,
places). The \texttt{lateral} stream ROI alone was reported as a sensitivity variant. RSA used all
voxels of the ROI (Appendix~\ref{app:nsd}, Table~\ref{tab:nsdsubjects}).

\paragraph{Patterns and pairs.} For $k = 3$ each image is represented by the mean of its three
presentations (primary); for $k = 1$ each presentation is used separately and the three RSA values
are averaged, excluding the pairs whose chosen presentations fall in the same session and run.
Model RDMs are computed as for THINGS (Section~\ref{sec:rsa}) from the own CNN at Conv1, 224\,px, and
from the ImageNet models with the layers of Section~\ref{sec:data}; RSA is the Spearman correlation
over all pairs.

\paragraph{Ridge.} The pre-specified ridge analysis of Section~\ref{sec:ridge} was applied unchanged
(nested cross-validation 8 $\times$ 5, the same $\alpha$ grid and selection, pooled out-of-fold $r$,
median over reliable voxels, the lower-edge gate), with the $k = 3$ responses as targets and
$g \in \{1, 4, 8\}$. A voxel is reliable if the mean $r$ of the three pairwise split correlations over
the 515 images, Spearman--Brown corrected as $3r/(1+2r)$, exceeds 0.2.

\paragraph{Inference.} Per subject, the contrast is averaged over seeds. Across subjects we used a
one-sided sign-flip test over the 8 subject means (256 assignments) in the predicted direction, and
a stimulus bootstrap of the 8-subject mean (1000 resamples, seed 20261002, the same image draws for
every subject). With 8 subjects the smallest attainable one-sided $p$ is $1/256$. The noise ceiling is
the leave-one-subject-out lower bound on the same pairs.
