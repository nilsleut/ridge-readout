In the pre-specified primary analysis (Section~\ref{sec:inference}), the untrained
(uncalibrated BN statistics) network scored below Backprop at every rung of the grid
ladder (Fig.~\ref{fig:prereg_ladder}). The untrained-minus-Backprop contrast in median
$r$ over reliable V1 voxels, averaged over subjects, was $\PreregRandBpGOne$,
$\PreregRandBpGTwo$, $\PreregRandBpGFour$ and $\PreregRandBpGEight$ at $g = 1, 2, 4, 8$.
The number of positive seeds was \PreregRandBpGOnePos, \PreregRandBpGTwoPos,
\PreregRandBpGFourPos{} and \PreregRandBpGEightPos{} of 5, and the one-sided sign-flip
$p$ was \PreregRandBpGOneP, \PreregRandBpGTwoP, \PreregRandBpGFourP{} and
\PreregRandBpGEightP. The pre-specified minimal detectable effect, derived from the
pilot's seed-level standard deviation (\PreregPilotSd), was
$\Delta_{\min} = \PreregDminSim$ (supplementary value from the primary analysis's own
seed variability: \PreregRandBpGFourDmin{} at $g = 4$ and \PreregRandBpGEightDmin{} at
$g = 8$), below the reference effect of $+\PreregReferenceEffect$. The criterion for
survival at $g \geq 4$ was not met. Because $\Delta_{\min}$ was smaller than the
reference effect, absence is informative under the plan, and the pre-specified rule
classifies this outcome as ``The effect disappears'' rather than ``The method cannot
resolve it''. In every seed the contrast was negative. With five seeds the smallest attainable one-sided $p$ is $1/32$ and the test addresses only the positive direction, so the negative direction is an observation, not a tested effect. Per-subject values are given in Appendix~\ref{app:persubject}; the mean over subjects includes sub-03, where ridge scores were close to zero. The four
secondary contrasts against Backprop (untrained BN-calibrated, FA, PC, STDP) were also
negative at every $g$; the largest was $\PreregSecondaryMaxMean$, and at most
\PreregSecondaryMaxPos{} of 5 seeds were positive in any of them.

In an exploratory check of this result (three subjects, Conv1, NATIVE 224\,px;
Appendix~\ref{app:ridgerobust}), the untrained (uncalibrated BN statistics) minus Backprop
contrast was negative in two further scores. In the MSE skill (one minus the ratio of the
squared error to that of the training-fold mean) it was $\FSkillMseSkillGOneMean$,
$\FSkillMseSkillGTwoMean$, $\FSkillMseSkillGFourMean$ and $\FSkillMseSkillGEightMean$ at
$g = 1, 2, 4, 8$, and in the per-fold $r$ it was $\FSkillPerfoldGOneMean$,
$\FSkillPerfoldGTwoMean$, $\FSkillPerfoldGFourMean$ and $\FSkillPerfoldGEightMean$ (mean over
subjects and seeds); no seed was positive in either score at any $g$ (F-R1 and F-R2: met). A
model that predicts the training-fold mean scored a pooled $r$ of $\FFloorMean$ (mean over
subjects); per-fold $r$ is undefined for predictions that are constant within each fold. Under
50 permutations of the stimulus assignment of the features (seed-0 untrained and Backprop,
$g = 1$ and $g = 4$), the 97.5th percentile of the null lay between $\FPermPNineSevenFiveMin$
and $\FPermPNineSevenFiveMax$; the observed scores in sub-01 and sub-02 (from $\FPermRealMinMain$
to $\FPermRealMaxMain$) lay above it (F-R3: met). In sub-03, described without a rule, the
observed scores (from $\FPermRealMinSubThree$ to $\FPermRealMaxSubThree$) also lay above the
97.5th percentiles of their nulls. A readout of each network together with the
luminance-and-contrast bank scored below the bank alone in all six conditions at $g = 1$ and
$g = 4$ (differences from $\FJointMinDiff$ to $\FJointMaxDiff$): for Backprop the joint readout
reached $\FJointBpGOneJoint$ and $\FJointBpGFourJoint$, against $\FJointBpGOneBank$ and
$\FJointBpGFourBank$ for the bank alone, and for the untrained network $\FJointRandGOneJoint$
and $\FJointRandGFourJoint$ (F-R4, joint minus bank at most 0.010 for Backprop: met). Adding
network features did not improve prediction; this does not show how much variance the
networks and the bank share. Absolute levels were low: the median $r$ of the best network
condition, Backprop at $g = 4$, was $\PreregCondBpGFour$ (mean over subjects); this correlation score should not be read as variance explained. The absolute MSE skill of Backprop at $g = 4$ was \FSkillAbsBpGFour{} and that of the untrained network \FSkillAbsRandGFour{} (median over reliable voxels, mean over subjects and seeds, relative to the training-fold mean).

Among the control banks, the luminance and contrast bank reached
\PreregCtlLumcontrastMeanGOne, \PreregCtlLumcontrastMeanGTwo,
\PreregCtlLumcontrastMeanGFour{} and \PreregCtlLumcontrastMeanGEight{} at
$g = 1, 2, 4, 8$, above the mean of every network condition at every $g$, including
Backprop (\PreregCondBpGOne, \PreregCondBpGTwo, \PreregCondBpGFour, \PreregCondBpGEight).
The pixel and Gabor banks ranged from \PreregCtlGaborMeanGTwo{} to
\PreregCtlPixelsMeanGFour. At $g = 4$ and $g = 8$ the intact grid exceeded its
image-wise cell shuffle for both the untrained and the Backprop seed-0 network in
\PreregShuffleWinSubjects{} of 3 subjects; this instrument covers one seed per condition
and is descriptive. The permuted-assignment control, the pipeline's negative test, for
which the plan expected a value near zero, gave \PreregCtlPermutedMeanGOne,
\PreregCtlPermutedMeanGTwo, \PreregCtlPermutedMeanGFour{} and

signal under the pooled out-of-fold correlation (Section~\ref{sec:ridge}), and the

\PreregLowEdgeHits{} lower-boundary selections at reliable voxels (Appendix~\ref{app:lowedge}) were classified as
plateaus (\PreregLowEdgePlateau), and no cell was excluded.

In an exploratory analysis of sub-01 (\NReliableSubOne{} reliable V1 voxels, $g = 1$,
NATIVE 224\,px), RSA and ridge disagreed in sign on the same voxels. RSA on the reliable
voxels gave an untrained-minus-Backprop difference of $+\RsaGapReliableGOne$; ridge gave
$\RidgeGapGOne$, and $\RidgeGapGFour$ and $\RidgeGapGEight$ at $g = 4$ and $g = 8$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:sign_flip}). Four candidate sources of the disagreement were tested
against rules fixed before each run, and none was supported. The voxel set: the RSA
difference stays positive on the voxels the ridge readout uses. The pooled out-of-fold
score: with per-fold $r$ the ridge contrast was $\ETwoPerFoldGap$ (a positive value would
have implicated pooling), and the two networks differed by \ETwoHiFracDiff{} in the share
of selections at the largest $\alpha$, below the threshold of 0.10 for a collapse
asymmetry. The fold convention: with run-grouped folds (Appendix~\ref{app:runfolds}) the contrast was
$\EThreeRunFoldGap$ (a positive value would have implicated leakage within runs). The
score: correlating RDMs built from the ridge predictions with the fMRI RDM gave
$\EFourEncGapRandom$ with random folds (\EFourEncPosSeedsRandom{} of 5 seeds
positive) and $\EFourEncGapRun$ with run-grouped folds. This meets the pre-stated rule
($\leq 0$), but the random-fold value is close to zero: the analysis shows that the RSA
advantage of the untrained network does not carry over to RDMs of ridge predictions, not
that it reverses there. In
the same subject, ridge did not separate the two untrained networks
($\POneRidgeRandBncal$; rule $\leq 0.010$: met), whereas RSA separated them by
$\POneRsaRandBncal$. In sub-02 and sub-03 the signs of the predicted pattern held: RSA
$+\STwoSubTwoRsa$ and $+\STwoSubThreeRsa$, ridge $\STwoSubTwoRidge$ and
$\STwoSubThreeRidge$. Sub-03 has \NReliableSubThree{} reliable voxels, ridge scores near zero in every condition and RSA and ridge differences close to zero; it contributes little to the pattern.
