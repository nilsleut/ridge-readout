In an exploratory analysis, each channel of the GAP features was z-scored over the 720
stimuli before RSA. In sub-01 on the reliable voxels (S1), the untrained (uncalibrated
BN statistics) minus Backprop difference became $\SOneStdGap$, below the threshold of
0.010 for the scale hypothesis, which was therefore supported; the two untrained
networks differed by \SOneStdRandBncal{} (control criterion $\leq 0.010$: met).

With the RSA convention of Section~\ref{sec:rsa} (all ROI voxels, three subjects,
stimulus bootstrap), raw features reproduced the published values (R1, met):
$+\VoneGapRawTwoTwoFour$ [$\VoneGapRawTwoTwoFourLo$, $\VoneGapRawTwoTwoFourHi$] at NATIVE
224\,px and $\VoneGapRawThreeTwo$ [$\VoneGapRawThreeTwoLo$, $\VoneGapRawThreeTwoHi$] at
32\,px. With z-scored channels the V1 gap was $\VoneGapZTwoTwoFour$
[$\VoneGapZTwoTwoFourLo$, $\VoneGapZTwoTwoFourHi$] at 224\,px (R2, upper bound
$\leq 0.010$: met) and $\VoneGapZThreeTwo$ [$\VoneGapZThreeTwoLo$, $\VoneGapZThreeTwoHi$]
at 32\,px. The resolution slope fell from $S_{\mathrm{raw}} = +\SlopeRaw$
[$\SlopeRawLo$, $\SlopeRawHi$] to $S_z = +\SlopeZ$ [$\SlopeZLo$, $\SlopeZHi$]
(R3, $S_z \leq 0.5\,S_{\mathrm{raw}}$: met; Fig.~\ref{fig:sign_flip}). At LOC the
Backprop-minus-untrained gap at 224\,px was $+\LocGapRaw$ [$\LocGapRawLo$, $\LocGapRawHi$]
with raw and $+\LocGapZ$ [$\LocGapZLo$, $\LocGapZHi$] with z-scored channels (R4, CI above
0 under z: met). With content limited to 32\,px (UPSAMPLED 224), the V1 gap was
$+\VoneGapRawUp$ [$\VoneGapRawUpLo$, $\VoneGapRawUpHi$] raw and $+\VoneGapZUp$
[$\VoneGapZUpLo$, $\VoneGapZUpHi$] z-scored (R5, upper bound $\leq 0.010$: met).

The moment ratio $m = \lVert\mu\rVert^2 / (\lVert\mu\rVert^2 + \sum_k \sigma_k^2)$ of the
channel means $\mu$ and SDs $\sigma_k$ tracks the difference $d$ between raw and
z-scored RSA: over 60 checkpoint-by-resolution cells of the untrained network and
Backprop, Spearman$(m, d) = \MOneSpearman$ (M1, threshold 0.6: met). For Backprop, $m$ was
ordered UPSAMPLED 224 $<$ NATIVE 224 $<$ NATIVE 32 in \PMechOneBpSeeds{} of 5 seeds, and
the untrained network's range of $m$ (\PMechOneRandRange) was below half of Backprop's
(P\_mech1, met). P\_mech2 was not met (Appendix~\ref{app:bandpass}): Backprop's $m$ was higher for band-pass inputs
without the coarse band than for the coarse band alone in \PMechTwoNotchSeeds{} of 5
seeds, but Spearman$(m, d)$ over all 50 cells including the band-pass inputs was
\PMechTwoSpearman, below 0.6.

Ridge on the same GAP features ($g = 1$) at every NATIVE resolution (P\_ridge; sub-01 and
sub-02) gave untrained-minus-Backprop contrasts of $\PRidgeGapThreeTwo$ at 32\,px and
$\PRidgeGapTwoTwoFour$ at 224\,px, non-positive at all six resolutions, with a slope of
$\PRidgeSlope$, below half of $S_{\mathrm{raw}}$ (met; Fig.~\ref{fig:sign_flip}).
