In an exploratory analysis, the euclidean RDM on unit-centred features was decomposed
into its norm component and its angular component (Section~\ref{sec:gainmethods};
Fig.~\ref{fig:separation}). Contrasts are untrained minus trained at V1 and trained minus
untrained at LOC; for the own CNN they are untrained (uncalibrated BN statistics) and
Backprop. The identity held to a maximum relative error of
\SepOneMaxRelError{} (SEP1, threshold $10^{-8}$: met).

SEP2 was not met: the V1 norm contrast ($F$ = center) was $+\SepOwnVoneNormCenter$
[$\SepOwnVoneNormCenterLo$, $\SepOwnVoneNormCenterHi$] in the own CNN, with a CI
including 0, and had a CI above 0 in \SepTwoModels{} of 6 ImageNet models. SEP3 was met:
the V1 angle contrast was $\SepOwnVoneAngleCenter$ [$\SepOwnVoneAngleCenterLo$,
$\SepOwnVoneAngleCenterHi$] in the own CNN and had a CI below 0 in \SepThreeModels{} of 6
ImageNet models. SEP4 was met: the LOC angle contrast was $+\SepOwnLocAngleCenter$
[$\SepOwnLocAngleCenterLo$, $\SepOwnLocAngleCenterHi$] in the own CNN and had a CI above 0
in \SepFourModels{} of 6 ImageNet models. Because SEP2 and SEP3 were not both met, the
pre-stated interpretation rule applies: the V1 ranking is not attributed to the norm or the angular component. On the angular component alone, the trained
networks were ahead at V1 in the own CNN and in \SepThreeModels{} ImageNet models, and
at LOC in the own CNN and in \SepFourModels{} ImageNet models.

The resolution slope of the contrasts was a descriptive point fixed in advance without a
decision rule. For the ImageNet models, the V1 angle contrast grew from 224 to 448\,px
with a CI above 0 in \SepSlopeAngleCiPositive{} of 5 models, for example
$+\SepSlopeResnetfiftyAngleCenter$ [$\SepSlopeResnetfiftyAngleCenterLo$,
$\SepSlopeResnetfiftyAngleCenterHi$] for resnet50; no norm slope had a CI excluding 0. For
the own CNN neither component had a slope from 32 to 224\,px under $F$ = center (norm
$\SepSlopeOwnNormCenter$ [$\SepSlopeOwnNormCenterLo$, $\SepSlopeOwnNormCenterHi$], angle
$+\SepSlopeOwnAngleCenter$ [$\SepSlopeOwnAngleCenterLo$, $\SepSlopeOwnAngleCenterHi$]).
convnext\_tiny was the exception among the ImageNet models: its V1 angle contrast
($\SepConvnexttinyVoneAngleCenter$ [$\SepConvnexttinyVoneAngleCenterLo$,
$\SepConvnexttinyVoneAngleCenterHi$]) and its angle slope ($+\SepSlopeConvnexttinyAngleCenter$
[$\SepSlopeConvnexttinyAngleCenterLo$, $\SepSlopeConvnexttinyAngleCenterHi$]) both had CIs
including 0. We have no account of this exception.
