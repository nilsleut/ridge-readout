\paragraph{Banded ridge.} A banded readout with separate penalties for the luminance-and-contrast bank
and the network (step 40) was meant to estimate the network's unique contribution beyond the bank. Its
sanity rule B1 was not met: the joint readout lay below the bank alone by more than 0.002 in some fits
(minimum $\BandedBOneMinGFour$ at $g = 4$), so B2 and B3 were not interpreted. A diagnosis (step 41)
showed no implementation error at the limit of an infinite network penalty (D-A1 met, maximum
deviation \DAOneDev), but a one-dimensional conditional search did not remove the deficit (D-A2 not met,
minimum $\DATwoMin$); the violation was therefore not attributed to selection variance, and the
conditional estimator was not interpreted. Descriptively, neither penalty scheme improved on the bank
alone in MSE skill (joint minus bank at $g = 4$: $\BandedUSkillBpGFour$ and $\CondUSkillBp$ for
Backprop, $\BandedUSkillRandGFour$ and $\CondUSkillRand$ for the untrained network), and in median $r$
the joint readout exceeded the bank by at most $\CondUBp$ (Appendix~\ref{app:banded},
Table~\ref{tab:banded}).

\paragraph{Repeated test images.} On the 100 test images, each shown 12 times, the paper cell did not
show the untrained advantage on presentation-averaged patterns (C1 not met: $\CvRsaVoneRawBOne$
[$\CvRsaVoneRawBOneLo$, $\CvRsaVoneRawBOneHi$]), whereas the standardised cell resolved a Backprop
advantage (C2 met: $\CvRsaVoneZBOne$ [$\CvRsaVoneZBOneLo$, $\CvRsaVoneZBOneHi$]); cross-validated and
plain Euclidean distances gave the same sign (C3 met). A noise curve over the number $k$ of averaged
presentations (step 41) showed that the contrast fell with $k$ (N1 and N2 met; Spearman of $k$ and
$\Delta(k)$ \NTwoSpearman), but the single-presentation value was not positive either (N3 not met:
$\Delta(1) = \NoiseVoneRawKOneFour$ [$\NoiseVoneRawKOneLo$, $\NoiseVoneRawKOneHi$]); under standardisation
$\Delta(k) \leq 0$ at every $k$ (N4 met; Appendix~\ref{app:banded}, Table~\ref{tab:noisecurve}). The
test images are z-scored with all trials like the 720 (T-global); their contrast under the other conventions
is reported in Section~\ref{sec:conventions}.
